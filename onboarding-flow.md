# Customer Onboarding Flow

**Category:** engineering

**Description:** None

## Instructions

New folk customer: 1) Sign up with email. 2) system creates tenant + folk_agent in Anigmae. 3) Sends welcome email from bizichatwill@agentmail.to. 4) Customer connects their apps (Google, etc). 5) System creates agentruntime with starter skills. 6) Customer's folk instance is live.
